**Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science and Engineering**  
and  
**Assistant Director (In-Charge), Research and Innovation Management Cell (RIMC)**

_Rangamati Science and Technology University (RMSTU), Bangladesh_

I am a researcher and educator working on **trustworthy and secure AI**. My research asks a simple question: *how do we build intelligent systems that stay reliable when the world, or an adversary, changes the data they see?*

My recent work focuses on **AI security and robust detection**: detecting previously unseen (zero-day) attacks through self-supervised and continual adaptation, defending generative models such as latent diffusion models against backdoor attacks, and reducing hallucinations in vision–language models. I am particularly motivated by the growing challenge of **AI-generated media and deepfakes**, and by detectors that generalize to new generators and real-world, compressed content.

Alongside this, I have a long-standing background in **computer vision, deep learning and Bangla NLP**, and I am exploring **federated learning and quantum computing** as emerging directions for secure and privacy-preserving AI.
